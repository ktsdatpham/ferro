# Ferro name and logo (draft)

The code is MPL-2.0: you may use, change and redistribute it under that
licence. The name "Ferro" and the Ferro logo are the project's marks (ADR
0010):

- You may say your product is "based on Ferro" or "compatible with Ferro".
- You may not call a modified build, a fork, or a service "Ferro", or use
  the logo, in a way that suggests it is the official project, without
  written permission.
- Plugins may use "for Ferro" in their names.

Registration of the mark is in progress outside this repository. This page
is a draft for review by a lawyer.
